# Slate Card · print set (Oct 2026)

Print pieces for Slate Kitchen & Cafe's loyalty card. All PDFs are print-ready at exact size.

- `Slate-Card-counter-sign-A5.pdf`: A5 sign for the counter or a stand.
- `Slate-Card-table-card.pdf`: 96 × 136 mm table card; `Slate-Card-table-cards-4-per-A4.pdf` prints 4 on A4 with 6 mm gaps to cut.
- `Slate-Card-handout.pdf`: 85 × 55 mm card to hand out with orders; `Slate-Card-handouts-8-per-A4.pdf` prints 8 on A4 with 6 mm gaps.
- PNGs are previews.
- **Loud set** (`genz.html`, `node render-genz.mjs`): the same three pieces in the Smash & Pass Gen Z style, in Slate red on carbon. Files start `Slate-Card-loud-`; `Slate-Card-loud-counter-sign-on-A4.pdf` puts the A5 sign on A4.

Brand: red #8F352D, cream #F6F5F3, sand #E8C9A0; Lilita One headlines, Cairo text and Arabic (fonts in `fonts/`).
The QR (`qr.svg`) opens https://slate.tapntreat1.workers.dev/t, the in-store counter link.
Edit `print.html` (open `print.html#counter`, `#table`, `#hand`, `#table-sheet`, `#hand-sheet`), then run `node render.mjs`.
