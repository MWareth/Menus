# Smash & Pass — Creative Partnership Proposal (REVISED)

Prepared by Marwan Wareth · 26 August 2026 · 17 pages
Source: `Smash_and_Pass_Creative_Proposal_REVISED.pdf` · verbatim text in `proposal-full-text.txt`

**The thesis:** build the system once, keep the brand hungry every month.
One-time foundation is separated from the recurring content engine so Smash & Pass
pays only once for the strategic setup. **Logo creation is excluded — the logo exists.**

---

## 1. Scope — two phases

**Phase 01 · Foundation + Launch (one-time)**
Brand guidelines (no logo creation) · menu design system · social template starter kit ·
22-item full-menu shoot · 4 launch videos · organized asset handover

**Phase 02 · Always-on Content (monthly retainer)**
Creative planning · one content creation session · 12 edited photos ·
4 short-form videos · 8 designed assets

## 2. What the brand needs

1. **Brand rules** — logo usage, color, type, voice, layout
2. **Menu hierarchy** — one structure for print, QR, delivery apps, updates
3. **Content language** — repeatable photography, video and copy direction
4. **Template library** — daily content without redesigning every post
5. **Launch assets** — a visual bank ready for opening and promotions

## 3. Brand guideline contents

- **Identity** — logo usage & clearspace, color hierarchy & contrast,
  Lilita One + Cairo typography, graphic devices & stickers
- **Communication** — brand personality, tone of voice, headline & caption rules,
  Arabic and English hierarchy
- **Application** — photography direction, video direction, social layout rules,
  menu and packaging examples

## 4. Typography + voice

> **Lilita shouts. Cairo explains.**
> A loud display voice paired with clean, readable information.

Taglines: `SAY LESS, ADD FRIES.` / `LOWKEY HUNGRY. HIGHKEY OBSESSED.` /
`DEE'SERTS ARE NOT OPTIONAL.`

Sample caption voice: *"You said one burger. We heard loaded fries and a
San Sebastian too. No judgement."*

## 5. Color + graphic system

> **Messy energy. Decent system.**
> Flat color, disciplined contrast and one chaotic element per layout.

| Butter | Ketchup | Carbon | Paper | Cobalt | Heat |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `#FFD21C` | `#F02820` | `#101010` | `#FFF9EE` | `#2452FF` | `#FF6A00` |

**Rule: one loud sticker. One strong headline. One clear point.**

## 6. Photography direction

> **The food should feel close enough to grab.**
> Direct flash, tight crops, real texture and saturated brand props.

Three words: **REAL MESS · HARD FLASH · TRUE COLOR**

**Production plan** — *Before:* shot list locked, props & backgrounds prepared,
food production schedule agreed, reference angles approved. *Day 01:* burgers +
chicken, fries + sides, hero combinations, backup selects. *Day 02:* pasta + mac,
pickups, short-form video sequences. *After:* selection within 48h, retouch +
color grade, web & print exports (~7 days; fast delivery reserved for launch).
Target is one day if all dishes are ready on schedule; day two protects quality.

`PINKY PROMISE` · `CRACK DEEZ`

## 9. Menu system

> **One master menu. Every customer touchpoint.**

- **Print** — master layout, editable prices & descriptions, clear category hierarchy
- **QR + mobile** — fast-reading item cards, mobile-first descriptions, add-on prompts
- **Delivery apps** — square image crops, short names & descriptions, consistent promo badges

**Menu design is a one-time deliverable. Price updates use the master.**
