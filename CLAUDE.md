# Menus — project context

Menu, brand and content design for **Smash & Pass** (Dubai smash-burger brand)
and its dessert partner **Dee's Treats**.

> Before creating ANY Smash & Pass visual, read `brand/PROPOSAL.md`.
> It holds the approved brand system, the visual direction and the commercial
> scope. `brand/proposal-full-text.txt` is the verbatim source and
> `brand/Smash_and_Pass_Creative_Proposal_REVISED.pdf` is the original.

## Brand quick reference

**Palette (six, flat, no gradients)**

| Name | Hex |
|------|-----|
| Butter | `#FFD21C` |
| Ketchup | `#F02820` |
| Carbon | `#101010` |
| Paper | `#FFF9EE` |
| Cobalt | `#2452FF` |
| Heat | `#FF6A00` |

**Type** — Lilita One shouts (display/headers), **Cairo** explains (body, and
Arabic + English hierarchy). *Note: existing menu board and deck files were
built with Archivo as the body face; the proposal specifies Cairo. Confirm with
Marwan before making new work — do not silently switch either way.*

**Voice** — "Say less, add fries." · "Lowkey hungry. Highkey obsessed." ·
"Dee'serts are not optional." Deadpan; the stickers do the shouting.

**Layout rule** — One loud sticker. One strong headline. One clear point.
Flat color, disciplined contrast, one chaotic element per layout.

**Photography** — Direct hard flash, tight crops, real texture, saturated brand
props. Real mess / hard flash / true color. Food should feel close enough to grab.

**Desserts** — Dee's Treats, 50/50 split. Written as `dee'sERTS` (Dee's mark +
"ERTS" in Lilita, brown `#543521`). Logo assets: `smash-n-pass-brand-deck/logo.png`
and `dees-mark.png`.
